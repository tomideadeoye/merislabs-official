### **EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW**

GK&A Logistics Services Ltd presents a **high-stakes opportunity** to partner in the operation and strategic scaling of the **Ikorodu Regional Inland Port**. Located at the Ipakodo/Ebute waterfront on the Lagos Lagoon, this facility is the critical multimodal alternative to the hyper-congested Apapa and Tin Can Island ports.

Operating under a **Public-Private Partnership (PPP)** framework with the **Nigerian Inland Waterways Authority (NIWA)** and in collaboration with the **Sealink Consortium**, GK&A has established a terminal that serves as a cornerstone of the **Customs Direct Ship Call** initiative.

### **COMPETITIVE MOATS**

#### 1. **Customs Direct Ship Call Status** ✅
GK&A operates under **Section 14 of the Customs and Excise Management Act (CEMA)**, granting direct customs clearance authority on-site. This eliminates intermediate bottlenecks and reduces cargo dwell time by an estimated **40–60%** compared to traditional port operators.

**Strategic Advantage:**
- Direct customs documentation and clearance
- Reduced turnaround time for importers/exporters
- Enhanced revenue collection transparency

---

#### 2. **Private 300-Capacity Truck Staging Facility** 🚛
The facility includes a dedicated truck holding bay with capacity for **300+ heavy-duty vehicles**, eliminating external congestion and enabling just-in-time cargo movement coordination.

**Operational Impact:**
- Eliminates port access delays
- Reduces fuel costs and driver downtime
- Enables scheduled cargo pickup windows

---

#### 3. **Multimodal Barge Advantage (Lagos Lagoon Connectivity)** 🌊
GK&A's Ikorodu location provides direct access to the **Lagos Lagoon inland waterway system**, enabling barge connectivity to Apapa, Tin Can Island, and eastern seaboard ports.

**Strategic Connectivity:**
- Barge feeder services to major container terminals
- Direct waterway access to industrial clusters
- NIWA-licensed inland waterway operations

---

#### 4. **First-Mover Advantage in Ikorodu** 🏆
GK&A is the **first private operator** to secure full regulatory approval and commence operations in the Ikorodu inland port corridor. This positions the company as the anchor tenant for future port-led industrialization in the region.
