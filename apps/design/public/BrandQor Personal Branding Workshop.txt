DELIVERY SPECS (global)
1. Slide format: 16:9 (1920×1080 px).

2. Slides total: 26 (numbered). Export as PDF + PPTX + editable Figma/Canva file.

3. Color palette: Dark charcoal (#0F1115), Off-white (#F6F6F6), Gold accent (#D4A76A), Teal/Accent (#0FA3A3) for graphs/CTA. Use the gold sparingly for prize elements.

4. Typography:

   * Headline: Mont (or Inter ExtraBold) — big, tight, minimal.

   * Subhead / Body: Inter / Roboto Regular (400) for readability.

   * Mono / small labels: Roboto Mono or Inter Mono.

      5. Imagery style: Cinematic, shallow-depth, warm light; hyphenate with grayscale backgrounds and a single warm accent on focal subjects. Realistic photos, tasteful portraits.

      6. Icons: Simple line icons (Stroke 1.5–2px). Use a consistent set (Feather / Heroicons).

      7. Graph style: Clean vector — avoid gridlines. Use the Flip L-graph as key visual; annotate with concise callouts.

      8. Animation (optional): Minimal, tasteful: fade-ins, slide-ups, simple chart growth reveal. Don’t over-animate.

      9. Accessibility: 18pt+ body text, 28pt+ headings. High contrast for legibility. Provide alt text for all images.

      10. Assets we need from you (provide these files to designer):

         * Hi-res portrait of Tobi (3 poses: headshot, 3/4 profile, candid working)

         * BrandQor logo (SVG + PNG)

         * 1–2 case study screenshots (Instagram growth, DM or testimonial screenshots)

         * LinkedIn profile screenshot (mock or real)

         * Any client logos you wish to show (transparent PNGs)

         * Any before/after metrics data (numbers to show)

            11. Deliverables: Editable source (Figma/Canva), PPTX, high-res PDF, single-page PNG hero image for social, separate JPG/PNG assets for the Flip L-graph and timeline.

DESIGN RULES (global micro-guidelines)
               * One main idea per slide. Keep copy to 5–10 words for headline slides; 12–25 words maximum for content slides.

               * Use visual hierarchy: large headline, medium subhead, small caption.

               * Use breathing space: min padding 64 px around content.

               * Use consistent grid: 12-col grid.

               * Use large, clear CTA buttons on offer slides.

               * Make the Flip L-graph slide the visual centrepiece: clean axes, bold curve, annotated breakout point.

               * Use subtle vignette on portrait slides to make text pop.
