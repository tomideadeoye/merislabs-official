# JEE Anniversary Logo Lab 2

This lab is strictly for the 30th anniversary identity.

## What changed
- Removed generic modes (`wordmark`, `monogram`) as primary options.
- Replaced with anniversary-first candidates only:
  1. `Heritage Seal 30`
  2. `Ribbon 30 Lockup`
  3. `Crest 30 Emblem`
- Kept objective stress tests: size, monochrome, blur.

## Decision rule
Pick the mark that keeps the "30" and anniversary signal clear at `24px` and still feels premium in monochrome.

## Recommended baseline
- Primary: `#121212`
- Accent: `#b81f38`
- Background: `#f6f3ee`
- First candidate to evaluate: `Heritage Seal 30`
